# Expert Review and Classification Interface

## What this application is

This application is a focused interface for collecting high-quality expert judgments on Q&A content.  
It helps research teams turn raw question-answer sets into a validated benchmark by combining review, classification decisions, structured feedback, and progress tracking.

## Who it is for

- Researchers and domain experts reviewing benchmark questions
- Project leads curating evaluation datasets for AI systems
- Teams building trustworthy QA benchmarks

## What users do in the app

1. Sign in and access a personal review workspace.
2. Review questions one by one, including:
   - the proposed answer,
   - supporting evidence,
   - reasoning behind the answer,
   - source citation (when available).
3. Mark each item as approved, rejected, or pending.
4. Leave review comments to explain decisions.
5. Track completion progress across the full question set.
6. Use the question bank to filter and revisit items by status and category.

## Core application outcomes

- **Higher data quality:** questionable items are flagged and corrected early.
- **Auditability:** every decision is tied to reviewer status and comments.
- **Faster curation cycles:** teams can monitor progress and focus effort where needed.
- **Benchmark readiness:** reviewed data is easier to trust for model evaluation.

## In short

This interface acts as the human-quality layer for QA benchmarks:  
it transforms a list of candidate Q&A items into a reviewed, defensible, and research-ready dataset.
